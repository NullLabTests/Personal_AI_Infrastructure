# 01_Cognition — Mental Models, Decisions & Daily Logs

**Purpose**: The executive center of the Life OS. Stores Daily Systems Reports, decision logs, chat histories with PAI/DeepSeek, mental models, and reflections. Everything here feeds into every other stack.

### What goes here
- `Daily-Systems-Report-YYYY-MM-DD.md` — daily wins, blockers, next actions
- `00_Chat-History.md` — context from PAI conversations
- `Decision-Log/` — dated records of significant decisions with rationale
- `Mental-Models/` — notes on frameworks, heuristics, systems thinking
- `Dataview-Example-Queries.md` — ready-to-use live dashboard queries
