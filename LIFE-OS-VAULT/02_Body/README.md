# 02_Body — Health Metrics & Data

**Purpose**: The sensory layer. Tracks all biometric, sleep, exercise, and bloodwork data over time. Enables correlation with cognitive performance (01_Cognition) and project energy (05_Impact).

### What goes here
- `Sleep/` — Oura/Whoop exports, sleep scores, HRV
- `Bloodwork/` — Lab results, biomarker trends
- `Workouts/` — Exercise logs, PRs, training plans
- `Metrics/` — Daily weight, HRV, resting heart rate CSVs
