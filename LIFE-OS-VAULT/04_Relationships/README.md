# 04_Relationships — People & Commitments

**Purpose**: The social layer. Tracks people notes, commitments, meeting logs, and relationship health. Ensures nothing falls through the cracks.

### What goes here
- `People/` — Per-person notes: context, preferences, history
- `Meetings/` — Logs of 1:1s, family meetings, calls
- `Commitments/` — Promises made to others, follow-ups
- `Annual-Review/` — Yearly relationship reflections

### Inputs ← 03_Capital (shared financial commitments) | Outputs → 05_Impact (collaborations)
